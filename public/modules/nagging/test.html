New Study: Simple Online Habits That Keep Your Personal Data Safe

By James Porter | March 11, 2026 | 5 min read
🔐

A new study published this week found that most personal data breaches could be prevented by following a few simple online habits. Researchers from three universities examined over 10,000 cases of data theft and found a clear pattern.

"The number one thing people can do is be cautious about what they agree to online," said Dr. Maria Santos, lead researcher at the Digital Safety Institute. "Many people click 'Allow' on popups without reading them just to make them go away."

The study recommends always reading popup messages carefully, saying "No" to notification requests from websites you don't fully trust, and being wary of websites that ask you the same question multiple times after you've already declined.
